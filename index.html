Rural Innovation Workshop

Workshop: Rural Innovation Workshop

Duration: 12–17 October 2026

Group: Group No. 4

Project: Lemon Size Grader

Guide / Mentor: Rajvee Ben Kalariya

Survey Location: Sarvedi Village, Bhavnagar District


Project Overview

Our project is a manually operated wooden Lemon Size Grader. It is designed to sort lemons into large, medium and small sizes using different-sized openings and a slope. The purpose is to make sorting and packing easier while saving farmers' time and labour.
