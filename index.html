06 · INNOVATION & TEAMWORK

Rural Innovation Workshop

A group project focused on a simple, manually operated lemon size grading model.

12–17 October 2026 · Group No. 4

Project: Lemon Size Grader

The project addresses the difficulty farmers can face when manually sorting lemons by size. The proposed wooden model uses a sloping surface and different-sized openings to separate lemons into large, medium and small categories, helping make sorting and packing easier.

Survey location: Sarvedi Village, Bhavnagar District, Gujarat

Guide / Mentor: Rajvee Ben Kalariya

My Role: Model Design

Estimated cost in proposal: ₹2,000 for one model; ₹20,000 for ten models.
